          NIL color.loadpalette 
          (
          )
Loads a palette of 17 basic colors, ready to use (see example). Colors are: 
red (255,0,0)
green (0,255,0)
blue (0,0,255)
cyan (0,255,255)
magenta (255,0,255)
yellow (255,255,0)
maroon (127,0,0)
grass (0,127,0)
navy (0,0,127)
turquoise (0,127,127)
violet (127,0,127)
olive (127,127,0)
white (255,255,255)
gray (127,127,127)
black (0,0,0)
orange (255,130,0)
chocolate (195,65,0) ReturnsNothing.
