          BOOLEAN power.lock 
          (
          )
Locks the power supply switch. 
Returnstrue/false Depending on whether the switch is blocked. 
NoteIf the switch is putted in stand-by mode, or you use one of those functions: power.suspend() or power.off() when the switch is unlocked with the power.unlock() the console will enter stand-by mode or will shutdown.
